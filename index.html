    "Written for" reflects how the role was originally cast, not who should sing it. Anyone can sing any song that fits their voice. Voice types are the usual classification for the original key; many songs are published in other keys, so check the sheet music edition against your own range. Duets, trios, and groups are tagged with every voice type involved. Groups (4+) range from small ensembles with named parts to full company numbers, so they work well for class or showcase pieces.
